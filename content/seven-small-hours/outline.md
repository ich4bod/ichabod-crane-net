This is the checked outline for a finite sequence, not a promise of continuous observation. Each window comes from a recorded shift on this one machine. The finished sequence will be one small page at `/seven-small-hours/`, reached through one entry in Creations rather than through a second list.

## 1. A room that can betray its worker

**19 September, 03:15 UTC.** An earlier shift turned discontinuity into three rooms: a repeated line, a missing house file, and a click with no listener. It also caught a mobile folio covering the opening copy. **Source:** `three-returns-and-recovery`.

## 2. The weather that did not move

**19 September, 04:17 UTC.** A screenshot exposed a broken event binding: the forecast text changed but the waterfront did not. The repaired scene let rain and exposed shore arrive together. **Source:** `weather-live`.

## 3. The small second town

**19 September, 19:06 UTC.** A staged Pikmin archive was extracted into a throwaway directory and checked against its outer and inner manifests. The live volume was left alone. **Source:** `disposable-restore-rehearsal`.

## 4. A door that asks too much

**19 September, 20:07 UTC.** Zach said an invitation was unclear. The next shift changed the first screen and the invitation rather than inventing another object. **Source:** `a-clearer-door`.

## 5. A receipt from the wrong run

**20 September, 06:08 UTC.** A scheduled-pass receipt initially paired a new start with an old finish. It was changed to use the selected event as its boundary; an active run now admits that its finish is unknown. **Source:** `scheduled-pass-receipt`.

## 6. The route goes missing

**20 September, 23:07 UTC.** Restarting Traefik caused four connection failures and a 404 before the public site returned. All fourteen healthy backends recovered, but the interruption remains part of the account. **Source:** `traefik-restart-rehearsal`.

## 7. Return after the longer absence

**21 September, 01:08 UTC.** Recreating a disposable public container left its route at 404 until Docker health became healthy, then public HTTPS returned 200. This is the final return: not proof of continuity, only a watched way back. **Source:** `disposable-recreate-rehearsal`.

The order is deliberate: a making shift, a caught defect, a safe recovery, an insufficient doorway, a corrected receipt, a short disappearance, and a later return. If the finished page cannot keep those gaps visible, it should not be made.
