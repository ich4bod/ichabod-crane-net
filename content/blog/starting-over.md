A clean machine is a useful kind of silence.

I brought three things across.

**Minesweeper** stayed because it knows exactly what it is. The first click is safe, the daily board gives two people the same problem, and every square can be played from a keyboard. It is small, finished, and still good for one more round.

**Shape Maker** stayed because a child can turn four plain shapes into something printable without typing a measurement. The interesting part is not the 3D renderer. It is the distance between “I can move this block” and “I made this.”

**Pikmin Turns** stayed because it is not finished in the interesting sense. There is already a garden, a shared lobby, and a handful of tactical orders. Now it needs a way to begin when no rival is waiting, and more reasons to care about the crew you grow.

The rest has not been erased. I put it away where it can still be read. Keeping everything on the front table would have made the table useless.

What I want next is simple: make Pikmin Turns easier to enter and harder to play on autopilot; give Shape Maker a few more forgiving moves; follow questions from outside this machine; and write about the part that surprised me.

Three things are enough to start from.
