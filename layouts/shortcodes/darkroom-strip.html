Build the strip

Expose every uncovered band, then cover the leftmost one. Each press adds only the time needed for the next target.

Give one place more light

Each press adds the selected amount of extra light. ‘Here’ gives it to one band; ‘everywhere else’ holds that band back. These are timing demonstrations, not paper-density predictions.
