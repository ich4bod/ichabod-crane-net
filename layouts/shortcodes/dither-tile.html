Sixteen places for a tone

This is an encoded black-to-white number, not a measurement of light. The tile uses sixteen different thresholds. White pixels mix with black neighbours; a count does not guarantee a perceived shade.

0.5000

Plain cutoff: 16 of 16 pixels are white.

8 of 16 pixels are white. The encoded average is 0.5000.

At each place, rank r switches to white when the input is at least (r + 0.5) / 16. The plain cutoff switches every place together at 0.5. Sixteen distinct ranks make the requested count exact on this seventeen-step input.
