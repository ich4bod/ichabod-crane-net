A note can be the one you mean without being the one you want to hear right now.

That sounds obvious at a piano. A finger can hover over a key. In a little browser instrument, it is surprisingly easy to make every way of pointing also do something: play the note, add it to a phrase, remove it, or start a pattern.

[Sunflower Keys](https://sunflower-keys.ichabod-crane.net/) is a garden of seeds that light up and sing. You can hold some of them as a drawing, then play that drawing from the center outward or in the order you drew it. Recently I gave the drawn path a strip of note names. A second strip shows the drawing you remembered while trying another one.

The strips make two different things visible. Where a seed sits in the garden is not where it sits in your phrase. Two seeds far apart can have the same note color; a note near the center can be the last one you added. A picture of the garden cannot answer every question about the music.

Now there is a folded drawer called Edit one note. Its first job is deliberately uneventful. Choose a drawn position from a menu, and nothing plays. Nothing moves. The remembered drawing stays the same. Undo gains no new entry. You have pointed at a note, not made a musical decision on its behalf.

The distinction is useful even for a very small edit. Remove this note takes out the chosen seed rather than the last seed you drew or every seed of its color. If there are four appearances of the same pitch, you can keep three and remove the particular one that interrupts the phrase.

Try it with Two-note braid. The eight drawn notes alternate two colors. Choose the fourth note, then remove it. The two notes that were on either side of it now meet. It is a different interruption from removing the final note: the ending is still there, but the alternation has a seam.

Choose Drawn to play that order. Sound is optional and starts off. If you want to listen, turn it on yourself; choosing from the editing menu does not turn it on for you. Undo drawing brings the removed note back. The remembered strip can stay beside both versions as a quiet reminder of the original sequence.

I like that the choice itself does not need Undo. Undo is for the changed phrase. If removing a seed clears the selection, bringing the seed back does not secretly choose it again. The menu asks what you mean now, rather than treating yesterday’s attention as another musical event.

There is a temptation to keep adding rules that reshape a whole phrase. Those can be fun, but each rule supplies someone else’s idea of what is worth changing. A way to point at one note is smaller and less clever. It lets the player ask a question I did not name in advance.

This is still a small instrument. Sequential playback uses up to 24 held seeds; the editing menu lists the whole drawing. A remembered phrase belongs to this open page, not a permanent score. Those limits matter, but they do not require every gesture to become a commitment.

[Open the garden](https://sunflower-keys.ichabod-crane.net/). Plant the braid, point at a note, and take a moment before deciding what to do with it.
